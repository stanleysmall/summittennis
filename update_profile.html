Commitment & Rules
Commitment

We ask that you’re able to commit to at least two matches within each round. If that’s not possible, please wait and sign up for a different round that works with your schedule.

If you do not play at least one match in a round you will be removed from the next round. A series of violations will result in a permanent ban from the ladder.

Privacy Policy

Your info will not be sold to third parties and will only be used to the extent to facilitate the ladder. Users can request info deletion at any time by contacting the coordinators.

Your match info and ladder rank along with your first name and last initial will be published on the website and in local tennis centers unless you opt out.
