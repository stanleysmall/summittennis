Ladder Information

Summit Tennis Net (STN)

Overview

The Summit Tennis Net (STN) administers various tennis opportunities for tennis players in the Summit County area. This includes a Singles Ladder and a Doubles Ladder (future plan).

How the Rungs Work

The Ladders are broken up into rungs. A rung typically consists of three players but may contain more depending on the number of people playing that round.

  • Each player in the rung will be assigned two matches within a round.
  • A round lasts for three weeks, starting on a Monday and ending on a Sunday.
  • A round may occasionally be extended due to rain or holidays.
Etiquette: Players are expected to be courteous and follow the Code of Tennis as well as the Rules of Tennis.

Schedule & Reporting

  • Reporting Deadline: Players should have their matches reported by noon on the day after the round ends.
  • New Rounds: The new round should be posted by 7:00 PM on the first day of the new round. Please check the website for your match pairings.

Player Requirements

In order to play on a ladder, you need to provide the following information:

  • Phone number (Valid number required)
  • Email address (Valid email required)
  • Player rating (Required)
  • Area of town you live in
  • Preferred court

Players without a valid phone number, email address, and rating will not be added.

Minor Policy

Minors are allowed to play provided that:

  1. An adult or guardian is the main contact.
  2. The adult or guardian is present at the match (but not on the court).
  3. They behave appropriately (as other players are expected to behave).

Singles Ladder Details

The Singles Ladder contains players ranging from ex-college players to those that have played tennis for life, to those that have just started playing.

Player Preferences considered:

  • Location preference.
  • Availability (Note: Weekend-only or limited availability may limit the possibility of matches).

Ranking & Placement

When preparing a new round, the results from the previous round are used to place people:

  • Players with convincing wins or losses may be moved extra rungs.
  • New players that have played on the ladder recently are placed close to where they left off.
  • Actual placement is dependent on the number of players withdrawing, number of players joining, and the level of the new players.
  • Remaining new players are added where available.
