Summit County Tennis Singles Ladder

Join the local tennis community for a flexible, competitive singles ladder! This is a great way to find new opponents and get consistent match play across the county without the rigid schedule of a traditional league.

View Ladder Rules Join the Singles Ladder

The Details

  • Flexible Scheduling: No set dates or times. You and your opponent coordinate your own matches at a time and local court that works for both of you.
  • The Ladder Format: Players are organized into rungs of three. You’ll compete against those in your rung to move up the ladder.
  • Match Frequency:
    • Winter (Indoor): 2 matches every 3 weeks.
    • Summer (Outdoor): 2 matches every 2 weeks.
  • All Skill Levels Welcome: Open to everyone! There is no minimum or maximum rating.
  • Fair Placement: Initial spots are assigned based on your USTA rating or a self-rating.
